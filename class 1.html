FETTUCCINE PASTA

RECIPE

PASTA RECIPE VIDEO

Ingredients

  1. –Butter 2 tbsp
  2. – Flour (maida) 2 tbsp
    3. +
  3. – 2 cups Milk
  4. – Salt-to taste
  5. – Black pepper ½ tsp
  6. – Optional : garlic, cheese

STEPS TO FOLLOW

Melt butter in a pan on medium heat
Add flour and cook for 1–2 minutes (don’t brown it)
Slowly add milk while stirring continuously (no lumps!)
Keep stirring until it becomes smooth and thick
Add salt and black pepper
Optional: Add garlic or cheese for extra flavor

  • How to Store ​​Fettuccine Alfredo

  • your leftover fettuccine Alfredo in an airtight container
  • It can stay up to three days.
  • Reheat gently in the microwave or on the stove.
